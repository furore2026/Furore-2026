Championship Rules

Contingent Guidelines

Institutions are invited to participate in Furore as official contingents, competing across four distinct clusters. Each event carries a star rating based on its scale, cash prize, and audience engagement, with points awarded to determine the overall championship standing.

Points Structure:

  • 5★ Events: 1st – 500 | 2nd – 250
  • 4★ Events: 1st – 400 | 2nd – 200
  • 3★ Events: 1st – 300 | 2nd – 150
  • 2★ Events: 1st – 200 | 2nd – 100
  • 1★ Events: 1st – 100 | 2nd – 50

Cluster-wise Discounts:

  • Sovereign Stage: 20%
  • Artisan's Guild: 10%
  • King's Council: 15%
  • Warriors Arena: 5%

Championship Eligibility:

To be eligible for the championship, each contingent must participate across all clusters, ensuring a balanced representation:

  • Sovereign Stage (Cultural): Minimum 6 events
  • Artisan's Guild (Creativity): Minimum 4 events
  • King's Council (Literature): Minimum 6 events
  • Warriors Arena (Games): Minimum 2 events

Step into the realm of Furore and represent your kingdom, as talent, skill, and ambition compete for the crown in a structured contingency system designed to reward both scale and consistency. The championship is not defined by a single win, but by a contingent's ability to perform, adapt, and excel across the realm of Furore.

Royal Decrees

Festival Guidelines

  • Valid college ID cards are mandatory for all participants and attendees.
  • Possession or use of narcotics, alcohol, or prohibited substances is strictly forbidden.
  • The judges' decision in all events is final and binding.
  • Participants must report at least 30 minutes before the event slot.
  • Any act of misconduct may result in immediate disqualification.
