About FURORE

Whispers of Royalty

FURORE is the annual cultural extravaganza of Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru — one of the most anticipated college fests in Karnataka. Every year, thousands of students from across the country converge to compete, perform, and celebrate.

This year's theme — "Whispers of Royalty" — transforms the campus into a grand medieval kingdom where every stage is a throne, every competition a battle for glory, and every participant a legend in the making.

From the thunderous crack of Battle of Bands to the razor-edged wordplay of Litsoc, from the grace of classical dance to the heat of a futsal final — FURORE 2026 is three days of unrelenting excellence.
